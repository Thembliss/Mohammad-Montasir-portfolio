Projects & research

Optimal Power Flow research, electrical design and embedded systems.

A Hybrid WOA–MFO Metaheuristic Algorithm for Solving Optimal Power Flow Problems BSc thesis

This thesis develops a hybrid Whale Optimization Algorithm–Moth-Flame Optimization (WOA–MFO) approach for solving the Optimal Power Flow problem. Implemented in MATLAB using MATPOWER, the proposed method is evaluated on the IEEE 30-bus and 57-bus benchmark systems. The study examines generation cost, convergence behaviour, solution quality and robustness across independent optimization runs, with comparisons against established hybrid metaheuristic approaches.

This paper presents a hybrid APO–WOA algorithm for solving constrained Optimal Power Flow problems, combining complementary search strategies and evaluating performance on benchmark power systems.
